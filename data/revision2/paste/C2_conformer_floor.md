## C2 — conformational floor (from existing QMugs data; no new calculation)

Across the 9,697 QMugs molecules with at least three conformers, the per-molecule standard deviation of the DFT HOMO-LUMO gap across conformers has a median of **0.076 eV** (IQR 0.028-0.160, 90th percentile 0.265 eV).

**Manuscript sentence.** Conformational variation alone produces a median spread of 0.076 eV in the reference gap, which places a floor on the accuracy any single-conformer model can achieve.
